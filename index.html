How to use

  1. Grocery List: tick the items you need, set quantity, and edit prices to match the shelf.
  2. Meals: browse recipes with ingredients and method.
  3. Summary & Total: see your cart, spending per category, print or export to CSV.

Prices are USD estimates, not live Spinneys/Carrefour prices. Edit them in the Grocery List.
